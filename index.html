FAQ

Common Questions

Is NELSON AI free to use?

Yes — you can start chatting right away with no credit card required. Some advanced features may have limits on the free plan.

Is my data safe and private?

Your conversations are tied to your account and are not shared publicly. See our Privacy Policy for details on what's collected and how it's used.

What makes NELSON AI different?

NELSON AI brings chat, image generation, voice, video, and offline AI together in one interface — built and maintained in Uganda.

Do I need to download anything?

No — NELSON AI runs entirely in your browser at nelsonai.netlify.app. No install required.

Does it really work offline?

Yes — offline mode runs a lighter AI model directly in your browser, so you can still get help without an internet connection.
